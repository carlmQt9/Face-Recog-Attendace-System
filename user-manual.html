1. Introduction

The DMCMES Smart Attendance System is built for Don Marcelo C. Marty Elementary School, Sta. Cruz, Zambales. It uses face recognition to mark student attendance in under one second and instantly notifies parents via email.

Key Features

Feature Description
📷 Face RecognitionAutomatic attendance — students look at the camera, done.
📧 Parent NotificationsInstant email on time-in and time-out events
👥 Multi-Role AccessSeparate dashboards for Admin, Teacher, and Student
✅ Approval SystemALL new accounts require admin approval before access
🟢 Welcome BannersPersonalized greeting with time-of-day on each dashboard
📄 PDF ExportDownload attendance reports as PDF directly in the browser
📱 QR CodeAlternative attendance marking using printed QR cards
🎨 Green ThemeUI colors aligned to the school logo (forest green + gold)

4. Getting Started

4.1 Registration

1
Go to the login page and click Register here, or click Register in the landing page navbar.
2
Fill in your Full Name, Email Address, select your Role, and enter role-specific fields (Student ID / Employee ID).

Attendance Methods

Once a session is live, there are 3 ways to mark student attendance using the toolbar buttons:

✨

Auto (Face)

Camera automatically detects and recognizes the student's face — fully hands-free.

👆

Manual

Teacher selects a student from the list and marks them present — for face/camera issues.

📱

QR Code

Student presents their printed QR card to the camera — scanned and recorded instantly.

Method 1 — ✨ Auto: Face Recognition

1
Open an active session and click the 📷 Camera button.
2
Click ✨ Auto in the toolbar (default mode). Students stand in front — the system auto-detects and records attendance.
3
A confirmation shows the student's name when recognized.
🖼️

📸 INSERT SCREENSHOT — Camera view in ✨ Auto mode showing the face detection overlay, scan line animation, and student name confirmation popup

Method 2 — 👆 Manual

1
In the camera view, click 👆 Manual in the toolbar.
2
A student list appears — search by name and select the student to mark present.
3
Attendance is recorded with the current timestamp and labeled "Manual" in reports.
🖼️

📸 INSERT SCREENSHOT — Camera view switched to 👆 Manual mode showing the student search list with names and a Mark button

Face Capture Page

When you click Capture on a student or teacher, a dedicated capture page opens with a live camera feed and a guided Liveness Verification process.

🖼️

📸 INSERT SCREENSHOT — Face capture page showing live camera feed with oval guide, "Position your face inside the oval" prompt, 4-step Liveness Verification panel on the right, Start Verification button, and Captured Samples thumbnails at the bottom

ElementDescription
Camera feedLive video from the device camera. An oval guide shows where to position the face.
"Position your face inside the oval"Instruction shown below the camera — align the face within the oval before starting.
LIVE badgeGreen badge — confirms the camera is actively streaming.
New Registration badgeYellow badge — shown when registering for the first time. Changes to "Re-register" if updating.
Captured Samples4 thumbnail slots at the bottom — fill up as each step is completed (1 per angle/blink).

Liveness Verification — 4 Steps

The system guides the person through 4 steps automatically after clicking Start Verification:

1
Face Front — Look straight at the camera. The system captures the front-facing sample.
2
Turn Left — Slowly turn your head to the left. Captures the left-angle sample.
3
Turn Right — Slowly turn your head to the right. Captures the right-angle sample.
4
Blink Detection — Blink naturally when prompted. Confirms a real person is present (prevents photo spoofing).

Buttons

ButtonWhat It Does
🛡️ Start VerificationBegins the 4-step liveness capture process. Make sure the face is well-lit before clicking.
← Back to listCancels and returns to the Face Registration list without saving.
💡
Best results: Good front-facing light, face centered in the oval, no glasses if possible, neutral expression. The person must be physically present — the blink step prevents using a photo.
📝
After successful registration, the student/teacher badge changes to Registered and they can now be recognized by the ✨ Auto camera mode.

8. Troubleshooting & FAQ

Common Issues

🚫 Face Not Recognized
Ensure proper front-facing lighting. Remove glasses/hats. Check camera permissions. Contact admin to re-register face.
📷 Camera Not Working
Check browser camera permissions (click camera icon in address bar). Close other apps using camera. Try refreshing.
🔒 Cannot Log In
Check email/password spelling. If yellow warning shows — wait for admin approval. Clear browser cache if needed.
📄 PDF Not Downloading
Allow pop-ups/downloads from this site in browser settings. Wait for "Generating..." to finish before clicking again.
🔑 Passwords Don't Match on Register
Watch for the live indicator below the Confirm Password field. Red = mismatch, Green = match. Form won't submit if mismatched.

FAQ

QuestionAnswer
Is facial data secure?Yes. Only mathematical descriptors are stored, not photos. Data is encrypted.
Can I use glasses?Yes, but register with and without glasses for best accuracy.
Why is my account pending?Admin hasn't approved it yet. Contact your school admin directly.
Can someone mark attendance for me?No. Face recognition ensures only you can mark your own attendance.
Can a new admin register?Yes, but they must wait for an existing approved admin to approve them first.
How accurate is face recognition?95%+ under normal lighting. Accuracy drops in dim/backlit environments.
What does "Method: System" mean?Attendance was recorded automatically by the system (face scan).
What does the green theme mean?The UI colors are aligned to the school logo — forest green + gold flame.
🛟
Still need help? Contact your school's system administrator or IT support.

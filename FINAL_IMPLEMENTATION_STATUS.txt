SYSTEM WORKFLOW
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

MORNING SESSION:
  1. Teacher starts session (session_type: morning_in)
  2. Teacher selects "📥 Time In" mode
  3. Students scan in (marked Present)
  4. Session ends
  5. System auto-marks non-scanned students as Absent

AFTERNOON SESSION:
  1. Teacher starts session (session_type: afternoon_out)
  2. Teacher selects "📥 Time In" or "📤 Time Out" (manual choice!)
  3. Student (was Absent) scans in
  4. Status auto-converted to Late
  5. Both times recorded (morning + afternoon)

RESULT:
  Student A: Present (In: 8:05 AM, Out: 5:00 PM)
  Student B: Late    (In: 1:30 PM, Out: 5:00 PM, was absent in morning)
  Student C: Absent  (No appearance)

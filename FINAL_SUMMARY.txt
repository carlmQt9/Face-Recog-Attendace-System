WORKFLOW
========
MORNING SESSION (morning_in):
  1. Teacher starts session
  2. Students scan → marked Present
  3. Session ends
  4. Non-scanned students auto-marked Absent

AFTERNOON SESSION (afternoon_out):
  1. Teacher starts session
  2. Student (previously Absent) scans
  3. Status auto-converts Absent → Late
  4. Both times recorded (morning and afternoon)
